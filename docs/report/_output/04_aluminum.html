4  Aluminium PFC

apps/aluminumNew — a compact PFC application in the same family as Chapter 3, parameterised for aluminium and built around a moving frame.

4.1 Physical setting

The same phase-field crystal picture as Chapter 3: a conserved periodic density whose minima are lattice sites. The differences are the equation of state, an FCC seeding initial condition (seed_grid_fcc), and a moving-frame formulation aimed at directional solidification, where the interesting physics is a front travelling through the domain rather than a bulk relaxation.

4.2 Governing equations

Conserved PFC dynamics, as in Equation 3.1:

\[ \partial_t \psi \;=\; \nabla^2 \frac{\delta F}{\delta \psi}. \]

The free energy has the same structure as Equation 3.2 — a quadratic correlation term plus a local polynomial in \(\psi\) and the mean-field density — with the aluminium coefficients \(\bar p_2,\bar p_3,\bar p_4\) and \(\bar q_{20},\dots,\bar q_{40}\).

4.3 Spectral formulation

\[ L(k) \;=\; u\,C(k), \qquad M_{\mathrm{nl}}(k) \;=\; u , \qquad u = k_{\mathrm{lap}} = -|\mathbf{k}|^2 . \tag{4.1}\]

Alongside \(C(k)\) the model carries a separate correlation kernel \(P(k)\) applied to the density each step. The pointwise nonlinearity is therefore a function of four arguments rather than two,

\[ N \;=\; N\big(\psi,\ \psi_{\mathrm{MF}},\ (P*\psi),\ T_{\mathrm{var}}(x,t)\big), \tag{4.2}\]

where \((P*\psi)\) is the kernel-filtered density — a convolution in real space, hence a multiply in reciprocal space — and \(T_{\mathrm{var}}\) is the imposed temperature field.

4.4 Moving frame and thermal drive

The temperature varies in space and time as a travelling profile,

\[ T_{\mathrm{var}}(x,t) \;=\; T_0 + G_{\mathrm{grid}}\big(x - x_{\mathrm{initial}} - V_{\mathrm{grid}}\,t\big), \tag{4.3}\]

clamped between T_min and T_max. A gradient \(G_{\mathrm{grid}}\) pulled through the domain at speed \(V_{\mathrm{grid}}\) is the standard directional-solidification setup: the front settles at the position where the local temperature matches the interface condition, and then travels with the gradient. Setting \(G_{\mathrm{grid}}=0\) recovers an isothermal run at T_const.

4.5 Solution strategy

ETD1 as in Equation 2.3, dealiased, with the mean-field filter and the correlation kernel evaluated as reciprocal-space multiplies. Two transforms per step plus the kernel application.

4.6 Verification

  • Regression. Pinned checksums for the shipped case across CPU and GPU builds.
  • Schema. JSON round-trip and range validation on every parameter.

Like Chapter 3 this model has no closed-form reference; coverage is regression and schema rather than analytical.

4.7 Binaries and inputs

aluminum_etd (CPU), aluminum_etd_cuda, aluminum_etd_hip. Preset inputs_json/smoke.json, plus aluminumNew.json.

4.8 Scalability

Not measured. The application shares the spectral ETD stack with Chapter 3, so its per-step cost is expected to track the tungsten curve at equal grid size, but that has not been verified and no curve is reported here.
